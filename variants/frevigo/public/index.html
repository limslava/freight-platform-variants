How a Guaranteed deal works

Money follows the cargo, not promises.

  1. Booking confirmedYou pay into the protected account. Funds are reserved, nothing is released yet.
  2. Loaded and departedBill of lading or rail waybill plus tracking confirm departure. The first part is released.
  3. ArrivedTracking and arrival notice confirm the cargo is at destination. The next part is released.
  4. DeliveredThe consignee confirms, or 72 hours pass with no claim. The balance goes to the provider and both sides rate the deal.

Payment details exist only inside {brand} — never in emails. Providers with a strong record receive more of the payment earlier.
